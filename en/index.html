Decide together.

Qohesion is open-source software for holding votes and polls in groups such as associations, unions and councils.

The software is under development. No vote can be held with it yet.

See the ballot screen preview Read the pricing

Three planned modes

The same software will run in three modes.

Mode Description
Light No account. A link is shared, then each participant confirms an email address and votes. For informal polls and consultations. Ballots are deleted on a set date after the poll closes.
Full web An account for the organization, with member management and recurring votes, on the hosted service.
Self-hosted The software is installed on the organization's own server, which sends email through its own service.

What exists today

  • The ballot screen preview, in two templates (see the preview).
  • The source code, under the AGPL-3.0-or-later, on GitHub.

Recording ballots and counting them are not written yet. Choosing an encryption method that third parties have audited comes before writing them.

Cost

The software is free and will stay free. The hosted service will be free for small polls, with an event pass beyond that (the prices are a draft).
